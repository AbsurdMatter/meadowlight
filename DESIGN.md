# DESIGN.md — Pillars & Mechanics

## Why this game exists

Cozy builders (Town to City, Station to Station) proved that relaxation and
building freedom sell. Meadowlight applies the same recipe to something
real: pollinator ecology. The player doesn't optimize a factory; they
steward a meadow — and the real-world lessons transfer to their own garden.

## Pillars

1. **Cozy, not efficient.** No fail state, no demands, no red numbers.
   Idle time is not wasted time — the meadow grows on its own.
2. **Freeform placement.** No grid snap. Winding paths, clustered flower
   drifts, and "messy" wild corners are the intended aesthetic.
3. **Ecology is the mechanic.** Every rule below maps to a real principle
   from EDUCATION.md.
4. **Learning is diegetic.** Facts surface when the player observes an
   object, never as quizzes or walls of text.
5. **Beauty is the progress bar.** No KPI dashboards. Progress = the scene
   getting more alive (color, bees, motion, sound).

## Core loop

Plant → attract → keep hives → observe → unlock gently → repeat.

## Mechanics and their ecological mapping

| Mechanic | Real principle |
|---|---|
| Flower diversity raises bee appeal | Diverse pollen/nectar sources support more species |
| Bloom succession across seasons | Single-species meadows cause feast-then-famine |
| Bee bath placement | Bees need shallow water; pebbles prevent drowning |
| Wild-corner patches | Unmown "messy" areas are the best habitat for wild pollinators |
| Bee hotel (sun-facing) | 70%+ of bee species are solitary; they need dry nesting holes |
| Wind shelter (hedges) | Bees waste energy flying in wind; sheltered plots forage better |
| No pesticides (implicit — nothing sprays) | Pesticide-free is a stated meadow identity |
| Hive grows slowly | A colony visits flowers at its own rhythm; forcing production kills |

## Season dial

A slow, player-controlled dial (spring → summer → autumn → winter). It is a
lens for understanding succession, not a challenge. Winter shows the hive
clustered and quiet — a teaching moment, never a loss.

## Milestone unlocks (soft toasts)

- 5 flower species planted → Bee Bath
- 10 species → Bee Hotel
- First hive fully healthy → Hedge/shelter pieces
- Wild corner survives a full year → rare pollinator (e.g., wool carder bee)
- Continuous bloom across 3 seasons → meadow expansion

## Feel

Warm palette (honey amber, sage green, dusty rose, sky blue). Golden-hour
lighting with long soft shadows. Flowers sway; bees buzz softly; wind
moves grass voxels. Everything at slow, low-frequency speeds.
